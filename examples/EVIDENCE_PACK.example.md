# Evidence Pack — private template

Store the real completed version under `private/EVIDENCE_PACK.md`.

## Experience

For each role, record dates, responsibilities, decisions, tools, and outcomes that can be defended
in an interview.

## Projects

Include only completed or truthfully described in-progress work. Distinguish prototypes, pilots,
and production deployments.

## Metrics bank

List the exact numbers that may appear in a resume and their source. Numbers outside this bank
should fail QA.

## Ban list

List titles, projects, standards, certifications, metrics, or claims that must never appear.
